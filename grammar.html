Grammar Lessons

Master essential Korean grammar patterns for TOPIK II

Level 3 Essential

-(으)면서

while / at the same time

Explanation

This grammar pattern is used to connect two actions that happen simultaneously. It means "while doing A, also doing B" or "at the same time as doing A."

Structure:

동사 + -(으)면서

Verb + -(eu)myeonseo

Examples

음악을 들으면서 공부해요.

I study while listening to music.

음악을 듣다 (listen to music) + -(으)면서 + 공부하다 (study)

한국어를 배우면서 한국 문화도了解하고 있어요.

While learning Korean, I'm also getting to know Korean culture.

한국어를 배우다 (learn Korean) + -(으)면서 + 한국 문화를了解하다 (understand Korean culture)

Practice

Complete the sentence:

친구와 대화하면서 _____________.

Level 4 Important

-ㄹ/을 뿐만 아니라

not only ... but also

Explanation

This grammar pattern is used to add information, meaning "not only A but also B." It emphasizes that both A and B are true or applicable.

Structure:

명사 + 를/을 + 뿐만 아니라

동사/형용사 + ㄹ/을 뿐만 아니라

Noun + (r)eul ppuman Ara + Verb/Adjective + (l/eul) ppuman Ara

Examples

한국어를 할 뿐만 아니라 중국어도 할 줄 알아요.

Not only can I speak Korean, but I can also speak Chinese.

한국어를 하다 (speak Korean) + -ㄹ 뿐만 아니라 + 중국어도 하다 (also speak Chinese)

이 식당은 음식이 맛있을 뿐만 아니라 가격도 저렴해요.

This restaurant not only has delicious food, but the prices are also affordable.

음식이 맛있다 (food is delicious) + -을 뿐만 아니라 + 가격도 저렴하다 (prices are also affordable)

Practice

Complete the sentence:

서울은 아름다울 뿐만 아니라 _____________.

Level 5 Advanced

-게 되다

to come to / to end up

Explanation

This grammar pattern indicates a change of state or a situation that has developed over time. It's used to express that something has come to happen or that a situation has changed.

Structure:

동사 + -게 되다

Verb + -ge doeda

Examples

한국에 오게 되어서 기뻐요.

I'm happy that I came to end up coming to Korea.

한국에 오다 (come to Korea) + -게 되다 (end up coming)

스마트폰 때문에 매일 인터넷을 사용하게 됐어요.

Because of smartphones, I ended up using the internet every day.

인터넷을 사용하다 (use internet) + -게 되다 (ended up doing)

Practice

Complete the sentence:

친구 때문에 한국 드라마를 보게 _____________.
